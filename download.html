🤖 How to install the MT5 Expert Advisor

  1. Tap MT5 Expert Advisor (.mq5) above to download it.
  2. On your computer, copy the file into:
    MetaTrader 5 → MQL5 → Experts folder.
  3. Open MT5 → Navigator panel (left side) → right-click → Refresh.
  4. Drag the EA onto your forex chart. Turn on Algo Trading.
  5. It starts with auto-trade OFF (signals + arrows only) — safer. Turn it ON only after testing on a demo account.
