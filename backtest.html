🔬 Backtest the MT5 Expert Advisor

Test the AI_Momentum_Trend strategy on your broker's real data before risking money.

Why backtest first?

A backtest replays the strategy over past data to show you its win rate, profit, and drawdown. Never trade a strategy you haven't backtested. This 5-minute check tells you if the settings suit your market.

Step-by-step in MT5

  1. Make sure the EA is installed (see the download page) and is in the Navigator.
  2. Open a chart for the pair you trade (e.g. EURUSD), pick your timeframe (M5/M15/H1).
  3. Go to View → Strategy Tester (or press Ctrl + R).
  4. Set the tester:
    FieldSuggested
    ExpertAI_Momentum_Trend
    Symbolyour pair (e.g. EURUSD)
    PeriodM15 (test M5 and H1 too)
    ModellingEvery tick based on real ticks
    Periode.g. Last 2–3 years
    Deposite.g. 10,000 (just for testing)
    Leveragee.g. 1:100
  5. Set AutoTrade = true in the Inputs tab (this is only for the test).
  6. Press Start and wait for it to finish.

What the results mean

Open the Backtest tab and check these:

MetricGood sign
Total net profitPositive
Profit factorAbove 1.5 (higher = better)
Win rateHonestly, ~40–60% is normal for this strategy
Max drawdownSmall relative to profit (e.g. under 20%)
Sharpe ratioAbove 1
💡 If the equity curve trends up smoothly → the settings work. If it swings wildly or goes down → reduce risk (smaller lot, wider stop), change the pair, or adjust the EMA periods. Test on 2–3 different markets.

GoalChange

⚠️ Past performance does not guarantee future results. Even a good backtest can fail live. Only go live with real money you can afford to lose, and use a demo account first. Not financial advice.
